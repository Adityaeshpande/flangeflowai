    Optional Phase 2 calculation

    Simple enough to audit

    01

    Target finished goods

    Confirmed demand + forecast demand + safety stock.

    02

    Recommended batch

    Maximum of zero and target finished goods minus current finished stock.

    03

    Gross material

    Batch quantity × BOM blank weight ÷ process yield.

    04

    Constraint check

    Compare required material with available material and production days with the planning horizon.
